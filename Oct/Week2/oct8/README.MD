# Day 8: Two Pointers from Ends

## Folder Contents & Summary

| Type | File | Approach | Time | Space |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary 1 | `valid_palindrome.py` | Use two pointers from opposite ends, skip non-alphanumeric characters, compare characters without case sensitivity, and move inward. | O(n) | O(1) |
| Primary 2 | `reverse_string.py` | Place two pointers at the beginning and end of the character array, swap the characters, and move both pointers inward. | O(n) | O(1) |
| Primary 3 | `reverse_vowels_of_a_string.py` | Move two pointers inward until both find vowels, swap those vowels, and continue until the pointers meet. | O(n) | O(1) auxiliary space |
